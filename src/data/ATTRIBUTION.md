## manatee-zones.json

- Layer: State Manatee Protection Zones in Florida
  `https://gis.myfwc.com/hosting/rest/services/Open_Data/State_Manatee_Protection_Zones_in_Florida/MapServer/9`
- Publisher: FWC, Division of Habitat & Species Conservation, Imperiled Species Management Section.
- Source last edit: 2025-05-13 (newest `last_edited_date`). ArcGIS item (owner GISLibrarian) modified 2026-03.
- Terms (ArcGIS item "licenseInfo", verbatim in substance): "Please acknowledge the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC) as the data source for any products developed from these data. Users should be aware that comparison with other data sets for the same area may be inaccurate due to inconsistencies resulting from changes in mapping conventions, data collection techniques, and computer processes over time. FWC shall not be liable for improper or incorrect use of these data."
- Layer disclaimer: "...for general reference only. In the event of conflict between this data set and the zones as described in the Florida Administrative Code (FAC) Chapter 68C-22, the descriptions in the FAC shall prevail. These state of Florida manatee protection zones may differ from zones created by City or County authorities and/or the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service."
- No named open licence (no CC0/CC-BY) is stated. Only an acknowledgement request and a no-liability clause. Treat as public-agency data, use with credit.
- Our transformations: clipped to bbox, polygons simplified (Douglas-Peucker, coordinates rounded to 5 decimals, about 11 m tolerance), fields normalised, waterbody derived by keyword from the description. Boundaries are therefore approximate. Do not present as the legal boundary; link to FAC 68C-22 (68C-22.006 Brevard, .007 Indian River, .008 St. Lucie).
- Source quirk: for 3 seasonal zones (ids 75, 79, 83) the source `DATE2_68C` ("MAR 1 - OCT 31") overlaps the first season; the text and FAC say "Nov 1 - Apr 30, unregulated otherwise". We follow the text/FAC (see `dataNote`).
- Pending rule change: FWC proposed/approved revisions to Indian River County zones in 2026 (public hearing April 2026). The layer may change; re-run the script and review the diff.

## boat-ramps.json

- Layer: FWC Florida Boat Ramp Inventory
  `https://gis.myfwc.com/mapping/rest/services/Open_Data/FWC_Florida_Boat_Ramp_Inventory/MapServer/4`
- Publisher: FWC (data compiled by UMass Boston Urban Harbors Institute, 2009; funded by a US Fish and Wildlife Service Sport Fish Restoration grant; maintained by FWC since).
- Source `last_edited_date` is 2026-09-30 for every row (bulk edit), so `lastVerified` means "last edited in FWC's database", not "re-surveyed".
- Terms (ArcGIS item "licenseInfo"): "Acknowledgement of the FWC-FWRI as the data source would be appreciated in any products developed from these data, and such acknowledgment as is standard for citation and legal practices for data source is expected by users of this data. Please cite the original metadata when using portions of the record to create a similar record of slightly altered data, such as reprojection. If any data are modified or adjusted, please share the edited information with FWC. ... FWC shall not be liable for improper or incorrect use of this data. These data are not legal documents and are not to be used as such. This is not a survey data set and should not be utilized as such. These data are not to be used for navigation."
- Again no named open licence. Note "share edited information with FWC" if we correct records.
- Fee amounts: units/period are not stated by FWC; verify with the operator before quoting prices.
